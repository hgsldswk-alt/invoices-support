Help · 使用帮助 · 使用說明

English

  1. Create a document. The app opens to a blank invoice on first launch. Enter your business, client and line items. Drafts save automatically, including offline. Set taxes, discounts or payment terms on the document.
  2. Send a PDF. Tap Preview to check the layout, template and color. Tap Send to attach the PDF in Mail. You can also choose Messages, the system share sheet or AirPrint as available on your device.
  3. Quote and record payments. In Estimates, open a quote and tap Convert to invoice to carry over its line items. On the invoice list, swipe left to mark an invoice paid and use Undo if needed. Open the invoice to record a deposit or partial payment and see the balance due. Optional overdue reminders notify you on your device.
  4. Keep a backup. Open More → Backup & export to export data as CSV, save a full backup or restore it. You can enable private iCloud sync under More → iCloud sync. Recently Deleted lets you restore documents for 30 days.
  5. Try or unlock Pro. The paid version gives you 30 days of Pro from first launch. If you claim the free trial in More → Pro, the original App Store transaction date determines the 30-day period. The trial costs $0 and has no automatic charge or renewal. Pro is a one-time purchase. Use Restore purchases on the same screen. After the trial, you can still create, view, edit, send and export unlimited documents.

简体中文

  1. 创建单据。首次打开即进入空白收款单，填写营业信息、客户和项目即可。离线也能编辑，草稿自动保存。可以在单据中设置税率、折扣和付款条件。
  2. 发送 PDF。点「预览」检查版式、模板和颜色，点「发送」可用系统邮件附上 PDF，也可以按设备支持情况选择短信、系统分享或 AirPrint 打印。
  3. 报价和记收款。打开报价单，点「转为收款单」即可沿用原报价项目。列表里左滑可标记已收款，误操作可撤销。打开单据可登记定金或分次收款，查看剩余应收金额。开启逾期提醒后，提示会发到自己的设备。
  4. 备份和恢复。在「更多 → 备份与导出」导出 CSV、保存完整备份或恢复备份。可在「更多 → iCloud 同步」开启私有同步。「最近删除」可恢复 30 天内删除的单据。
  5. 试用或买断高级版。收费版首次启动可体验高级功能 30 天。若在「更多 → 高级版」领取免费试用，30 天按 App Store 原始交易日期计算。试用费用为 0 元，到期无自动扣费或续期。高级功能可一次买断，也可在同一页恢复购买。试用到期后仍可不限张创建、查看、编辑、发送和导出单据。

繁體中文

Privacy Policy · 隐私政策 · 隱私權政策

English

The developer does not collect personal data through the app. The app has no developer account, backend, ads, analytics or tracking. Documents, client details, payment records, photos and signatures are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple syncs this data through your private iCloud database. The developer has no access to it.

The system contact and photo pickers provide only the entries and photos you choose. Camera access is used to take photos for attachments. Local notifications remind you about due dates and recurring drafts. The optional Face ID lock uses Apple’s authentication and does not give the app access to facial data.

You choose where to send PDFs and backups. Your chosen mail, messaging, sharing or printing service handles the files you send. Payment links and QR codes use information you enter. The app does not process your clients’ payments.

Apple handles App Store purchases. The app uses StoreKit to verify and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment details. Deleted documents can be recovered from Recently Deleted for 30 days. Copies you send or export are managed by you and the recipient or service.
